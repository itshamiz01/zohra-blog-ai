# Content Gap & Information Gain Engine

## Why Word Count is Not an SEO Strategy

Writing 4,000 words when competitors write 2,000 words is content padding, not information gain.
Zohra identifies **structural dimensions** missing across top search results.

---

## Recognized Content Gap Types

| Gap Type | Description | Importance |
|---|---|---|
| `missing_pricing` | Competitors mention plans exist but omit exact tiers, monthly billing, or free-tier limits | High (85/100) |
| `lack_of_workflow` | High-level promotional summaries without step-by-step implementation code or workflows | Critical (90/100) |
| `no_limitations` | Biased promotional content failing to outline real-world drawbacks, compute bottlenecks, or downsides | Medium-High (75/100) |
| `regional_gap` | Zero information on pricing or availability in specific regions (e.g., India UPI support, EU GDPR limits) | High (80/100) |
| `weak_comparisons` | Narrative text without a clear side-by-side benchmark comparison matrix | High (85/100) |

---

## Information Gain Score

$$\text{Information Gain Score} = 0.60 \times \text{SERP Gap Score} + 0.40 \times \text{Competition Opportunity Score}$$

Feeds into `original_value_score` to prioritize topics where Zohra can produce genuinely novel value.
